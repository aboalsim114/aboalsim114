# Niche professional / small-business workflows still on paper, Excel or WhatsApp (France/Europe, 2024-2026)

Research scope: B2B / professional niches (artisans, associations, small shops, farmers, micro-entrepreneurs, clubs, market traders). Consumer and health/caregiving are excluded. Research date: 16 Nov 2026. About 20 tool calls. Several primary sites could not be fetched from this session (reussir.fr, questions.assemblee-nationale.fr, blogdumoderateur.com were blocked by the network), so some findings rely on search-result summaries and are flagged.

**Bottom line for the report writer:** many of the "obvious" niches (artisan quotes and invoices, HACCP for restaurants, e-invoicing for micro-entrepreneurs, beekeeping registers, sports club management) are already crowded with cheap or free tools. The strongest remaining gaps are **mandatory registers that the law imposes on very small structures**: the used-goods police register (livre de police), the register of exhibitors at vide-greniers, the digital phytosanitary register due in 2027 for small or diversified farms, and the placier workflow in small communes. Ten niches are assessed below, each with a verdict.

## 1. Which professions/organizations still rely on spreadsheets, paper or messaging apps for core workflows, and what do they complain about?

### Takeaway
Digital adoption among French very small businesses (TPE) is rising, but it is uneven by sector and size. The clearest remaining paper workflows are **legal registers** (used goods, vide-greniers, phytosanitary, beekeeping, market receipts) and **volunteer coordination** in associations and clubs. I found few real forum or Reddit complaints. Most of the evidence comes from regulations, municipal rules and vendor pages.

### Cited Findings
**Macro context**
- France Num baromètre 2025 (6th edition): 11,021 firms surveyed, including 7,878 TPE (26 March to 18 April 2025). 78% of owners say digital tools bring real benefits. AI use rose from 5% (2023) to 26% (2025). The study notes a persistent gap between sectors and company sizes. The CREDOC ran 31 qualitative interviews covering e-invoicing and digital skills — [entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/espace-presse/france-num-presente-la-6e-edition-de-son-barometre-annuel-sur-la-transformation); [francenum.gouv.fr](https://www.francenum.gouv.fr/guides-et-conseils/strategie-numerique/comprendre-le-numerique/barometre-france-num-2025-resultats). A 2026 edition (about 9,000 firms, published 28/09/2026) exists but I did not verify its figures — [economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/node/3242215)
- There were 3.186M administratively active micro-entrepreneurs at end of June 2025 (+204k in a year). Only 49.8% (about 1.59M) declared positive turnover in Q2 2025, for a total of €8.05bn in that quarter — [tpeactu.fr summarising URSSAF](https://tpeactu.fr/2026/01/29/auto-entrepreneurs-chiffre-affaires-urssaf-2025/)
- A TechRadar-cited report says 90% of organisations still rely on spreadsheets for some vital data. This is generic and not TPE-specific — [techradar.com](https://techradar.com/pro/almost-all-firms-are-still-reliant-on-spreadsheets-is-it-time-for-change)
- Associations: Recherches & Solidarités and Solidatech published "La place du numérique dans le projet associatif" in 2025, a triennial survey. It finds that digital usage keeps growing but that tools remain under-used. I found no statistic on the share of associations using Excel or WhatsApp for volunteers — [R&S Solidatech synthèse 2025](https://recherches-solidarites.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/RS-Solidatech-Synthese.pdf); [CP 2025](https://recherches-solidarites.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CP-RS-Solidatech-2025.pdf)

**Paper-bound legal registers (strongest signal)**
- **Used-goods register (registre des objets mobiliers, "livre de police").** It is mandatory for brocanteurs, antiquaires and dépôt-vente. Each object gets a sequential number that must also be shown on the object. Having no register, an inaccurate one, or refusing to show it is a criminal offence (délit) — [Bpifrance Création](https://bpifrance-creation.fr/activites-reglementees/antiquaire-brocanteur); [entreprendre.service-public.fr](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F39552?profil=tout). Paper registers are still sold as a standard product, for example the Exacompta 100-page "spécial brocanteurs et antiquaires" register at La Poste — [laposte.fr](https://www.laposte.fr/boutique/registre-32x24cm-objets-mobiliers-special-brocanteurs-et-antiquaires-100-pages-bleu-exacompta/p/mp-500021061). It can be kept electronically if it is reliable and can serve as proof, and it must be kept 5 years — [copeps.fr](https://copeps.fr/actualites/auto-entrepreneur-livre-de-police/); [creerentreprise.fr](https://www.creerentreprise.fr/livre-de-police-registre-objects-mobiliers-definition-reglementation-fonctionnement/). One source cites a fine of up to €30,000 (secondary source, not verified against the Code pénal).
- **Vide-greniers run by associations.** Organisers must keep a register identifying each seller (name, address, ID, nature of goods). Each private seller must sign a sworn statement that they have not taken part in more than 2 similar events that year. A prior declaration (vente au déballage) is due 15 days before the event — [associatheque.fr](https://www.associatheque.fr/fr/organiser-une-manifestation/vide-greniers.html); [Ploemeur mémento organisateur 2026](https://www.ploemeur.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026-07-21-memento-organisateur-vente-deballage.pdf). Registrations often go through a website form or HelloAsso, with some on-site slots kept — [HelloAsso example](https://www.helloasso.com/associations/association-les-4m/evenements/vide-grenier-2026).
- **Outdoor markets (marchés forains).** The placier assigns free spots to "passagers" on the day, sometimes by drawing lots — [Givors règlement](https://www.givors.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/REGLEMENT-MARCHES.pdf); [connect.saint-etienne.fr](https://connect.saint-etienne.fr/demarche/256/4). Non-subscribers may get receipts torn from a stub book (carnet à souches) kept by the régisseur placier — [Villedenay règlement](https://villedenay.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/2209-Reglement_marche_VD_signe.pdf). Each payment must produce a receipt showing commune, date, trader and metres occupied — [justice.fr](https://www.justice.fr/fiche/demander-emplacement-marche-halle).
- **Beekeepers.** They must keep a breeding register (treatments, hive movements) and a cahier de miellerie (harvests, lot numbers) — [espace-autoentrepreneur.com](https://espace-autoentrepreneur.com/ruche/article/devenir-apiculteur-autoentrepreneur).
- **Restaurants (HACCP).** No law requires digital records, and a paper binder is still accepted — [lafabriquedunet.fr](https://www.lafabriquedunet.fr/logiciels/gestion/logiciel-haccp).

**Volunteer coordination (clubs and associations)**
- One sports-software vendor tells of a club official who handled the call-ups of referees and scorers (officiels de table de marque) alone for more than 20 years and wanted a simple tool to hand the job over. This is a vendor anecdote — [scorenco.com](https://scorenco.com/clubs/)
- Federation guides recommend planning volunteers for events and the buvette, and note that a club's leadership is usually 5 to 8 volunteers — [district-foot-lot.fff.fr](https://district-foot-lot.fff.fr/wp-content/uploads/sites/71/bsk-pdf-manager/ee4bd543e2d2885344706b8b6a3ced7f.pdf); [abargenteuil.fr](https://abargenteuil.fr/blog/heure-du-match-boisson-gauff/)

### Inferences
- The best signal of "still on paper" is a legal obligation that is cheap to meet on paper (a register bought at La Poste, a stub book) but painful in practice: handwriting at 6 a.m. on a market, police checks, the yearly limit of 2 participations, conversion within 30 days.
- These users are small, part-time or volunteer-run. They have low willingness to pay (WTP), so pricing must sit around €0-10 a month, or be paid by the organiser, the commune or a federation.

### Gaps
- I found no first-hand Reddit or forum threads (r/smallbusiness, r/freelance, French trade forums) with quoted complaints. Searches returned SEO and vendor content. Field interviews or Facebook trade groups would be needed.
- The France Num 2025 detailed figures by sector (share of TPE with management or invoicing software) could not be fetched.

## 2. Upcoming regulatory changes creating new needs — which segments are left without affordable tools?

### Takeaway
Two deadlines matter. **E-invoicing**: every business must be able to receive e-invoices since 1 Sept 2026, and TPE and micro-entrepreneurs must issue them from 1 Sept 2027. **The EU digital phytosanitary register**: from 1 Jan 2027, with a transition until end 2029. E-invoicing is now well covered by free platforms. The phyto register is the more open gap, especially for small or diversified farms.

### Cited Findings
**E-invoicing (France)**
- Receiving e-invoices has been mandatory for all since 1 Sept 2026, including micro-entrepreneurs under the VAT franchise. Issuing and e-reporting become mandatory on 1 Sept 2027 for TPE and micro-enterprises (Sept 2026 for large companies and ETI) — [abby.fr](https://abby.fr/guide/facturation-electronique/auto-entrepreneur-2026); [tide.co](https://www.tide.co/fr-FR/blog/facturation-et-tresorerie/facturation-electronique/)
- The free public platform (PPF) was abandoned as a deposit platform, so businesses must use an approved private platform (plateforme agréée). A French parliamentary written question says this abandonment puts micro-enterprises and TPE in a difficult position — [abby.fr](https://abby.fr/guide/facturation-electronique/facturation-electronique-calendrier); [Assemblée nationale QE 10484](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/questions/QANR5L17QE10484.pdf) (known only from the search snippet; fetch blocked)
- Several approved platforms offer free plans for micro-entrepreneurs: Pennylane, Qonto, Indy, Tiime, Abby — [tide.co](https://www.tide.co/fr-FR/blog/facturation-et-tresorerie/facturation-electronique-auto-entrepreneur/); [indy.fr](https://www.indy.fr/guide/facturation/electronique/qui-est-concerne/). Costructor offers free quotes and invoices for building trades — [tool-advisor.fr](https://tool-advisor.fr/logiciel-facturation/comparatif/costructor/)
- Readiness surveys disagree:
  - Tiime/OpinionWay (607 owners of firms under 20 staff): 86% have heard of the reform, but only 35% know its content precisely — [blog.tiime.fr](https://blog.tiime.fr/facturation-electronique-2026-etude-tpe-france)
  - Qonto/OpinionWay (March 2026): only 18% of TPE-PME were equipped — [search summary]
  - OpinionWay for the Ordre des experts-comptables and ECMA: 38% of firms not ready — [lejournaldesentreprises.com](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/facturation-electronique-38-des-entreprises-ne-sont-pas-pretes-mais-letat-fera-preuve-de-tolerance-2146577)
  - LegalPlace (more than 1,500 TPE owners, about a month after 1 Sept 2026): 71% have chosen a platform, but 1 in 5 has not started — [legalplace.fr](https://www.legalplace.fr/actualites/barometre-legalplace-facturation-electronique/)
  - Sage/OpinionWay (July 2026): 92% "ready", but the scope is unclear — [sage.com](https://www.sage.com/fr-fr/actualites/communiques-presse/2026/07/barometre-opinionway-facture-electronique/)
  - Cerfrance/Ifop (June 2026, 600 owners): among those whose transition is unfinished, only 40% know the support options — [cerfrance.fr](https://www.cerfrance.fr/upload/files/document/6a2133d90123a5.43134378_20260601_Communique_de_presse_Cerfrance_Etude_Ifop_1.pdf)
- Higher penalties were reported (€50 per non-compliant invoice, €500 per missing e-reporting, €500 for having no platform). This appears in only one secondary source (Tide) and is not verified — [tide.co](https://www.tide.co/fr-FR/blog/facturation-et-tresorerie/facturation-electronique/)

**Digital phytosanitary register (EU)**
- EU Implementing Regulation 2023/564 requires records to be kept in a machine-readable electronic format. Regulation 2025/2203 postponed this from 1 Jan 2026 to 1 Jan 2027 — [EUR-Lex 2025/2203](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/2203/oj/eng); [terre-net.fr](https://www.terre-net.fr/produits-phytos/article/905029/registre-phytosanitaire-numerique-etes-vous-pret-pour-2027)
- France applies it through a ministerial order (arrêté) of 24 Dec 2025, published in the JO of 31 Dec 2025 — [aida.ineris.fr](https://aida.ineris.fr/node/45236)
- No software is imposed, and a spreadsheet can count as a digital register. Records not kept digitally must be converted. Sources conflict on the deadline: 30 days after use (EU text) or 31 January of the following year (Reussir summary). Paper entry remains tolerated during a transition from 2027 to end 2029 — [corteva.com](https://www.corteva.com/fr/cultures/toutes-cultures/registre-phyto-numerique-ce-que-change-vraiment-le-report.html); [pleinchamp.com](https://www.pleinchamp.com/actualite/l-application-du-registre-phytosanitaire-numerique-reportee-au-1er-janvier-2027)
- Required data per treatment: product name and AMM number, date and time, treated area, parcel location (RPG reference or GPS) and crop — [terre-net.fr](https://www.terre-net.fr/produits-phytos/article/905029/registre-phytosanitaire-numerique-etes-vous-pret-pour-2027)
- Chambres d'agriculture France is developing an application at the ministry's request. It has been tested with a farmer panel since October, with rollout from Jan 2027. Its price is unknown. Farms already on Geofolia, Mes Parcelles, Smag or Farmi are "little impacted" — [search summary of reussir.fr](https://www.reussir.fr/articles/registre-phytosanitaire-ce-qui-change-au-1er-janvier-2027) (fetch blocked)
- Belgium (AFSCA) issued its own circular on the electronic register in Dec 2025, so the need applies EU-wide — [favv-afsca.be](https://favv-afsca.be/sites/default/files/circ-omz/676013/20251217_FR_TC_circ_register_PPP.pdf)

### Inferences
- **E-invoicing is mostly solved for micro-entrepreneurs** by free offers, often bundled with a bank account. A new entrant would fight Qonto, Pennylane and Indy on price zero. Gaps that may remain: (a) older or low-digital artisans who invoice on paper or in Word and need a "photo or voice to Factur-X" assistant; (b) B2C e-reporting from Sept 2027 for market traders and small shops who take cash. This is speculative, and the existing free tools may add it.
- **The phyto register is the more open regulatory opportunity in Europe**. The incumbents (Smag, Geofolia, Mes Parcelles) are farm-management suites built for field crops. The users most likely left on paper are small market gardeners, small winegrowers, diversified or organic farms and non-farm professional users. They need an offline, mobile, voice- or photo-based entry tool. Risk: the free Chambres d'agriculture app may close the gap.

### Gaps
- The exact price of the Chambres d'agriculture app and of Mes Parcelles: not found.
- The number of French farms still keeping a paper phyto register: not found.
- The official text on penalties and the B2C e-reporting details for micro-entrepreneurs: not verified from a primary source.

## 3. For each niche: existing tools, why they fall short, MVP idea, willingness to pay (WTP) and market size

### Takeaway
Of the 10 niches assessed, 4 look promising (A-D), 2 are possible but harder (E-F), and 4 are crowded and not recommended (G-J). Willingness to pay is low everywhere (€0-30 a month), so freemium plus payment by the organiser or federation is the realistic model. Where possible, revenue should come from the event or the transaction rather than from a subscription.

### Cited Findings and per-niche analysis

**A. Digital used-goods register (livre de police) for brocanteurs, dépôts-vente, second-hand resellers and vintage auto-entrepreneurs — PROMISING**
- Pain: the register is mandatory and a criminal offence if absent or wrong. Every object needs a number, a seller identity and a price. Paper registers are still sold widely, which suggests strong paper use — [Bpifrance Création](https://bpifrance-creation.fr/activites-reglementees/antiquaire-brocanteur); [laposte.fr](https://www.laposte.fr/boutique/registre-32x24cm-objets-mobiliers-special-brocanteurs-et-antiquaires-100-pages-bleu-exacompta/p/mp-500021061)
- Existing tools: Tracely, a second-hand suite, costs €29.90 or €39.90 per user per month after a 15-day trial — [appvizer.fr](https://www.appvizer.fr/operations/gestion-entreprise/tracely). KeplerVO and AutoCopilot (from €39/month) target used cars — [autocopilot.fr](https://autocopilot.fr/guides/logiciel-gestion-vo). EBP point-of-sale bundles are another option. A digital register has reportedly been allowed since 2013, under proof and reliability conditions — [creerentreprise.fr](https://www.creerentreprise.fr/livre-de-police-registre-objects-mobiliers-definition-reglementation-fonctionnement/)
- Gap: about €30 per user per month is high for part-time brocanteurs and vintage resellers on Vinted or Leboncoin. I found no cheap, mobile-first tool built around photos.
- MVP: a phone app where you photograph the object and the seller's ID card (with OCR), get an automatic sequential number, print a label or QR code, keep a tamper-evident log (timestamp and hash chain), and export a PDF for police checks. Price: €5-9 a month.
- Market size: not found. The number of registered revendeurs d'objets mobiliers is unknown (gap).

**B. Vide-greniers and brocantes organised by associations, comités des fêtes, school PTAs and clubs — PROMISING**
- Pain: a legal register of sellers, a sworn statement per private seller (maximum 2 events a year), a declaration to the mairie 15 days before, a stand plan, payments, and morning check-in. All of this is usually paper forms plus HelloAsso for payment — [associatheque.fr](https://www.associatheque.fr/fr/organiser-une-manifestation/vide-greniers.html); [Ploemeur mémento 2026](https://www.ploemeur.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026-07-21-memento-organisateur-vente-deballage.pdf); [Allauch dossier brocante assoc](https://www.allauch.com/fileadmin/Documents/Que_faire_a_allauch/dossier_brocante_assoc.pdf)
- Existing tools: HelloAsso (free, handles ticketing and payment, not the legal register or stand plan). Brocabrac and MyBrocante publish event rules and documents, and one search result says an online booking tool can automate registrations, plans and payments — [mybrocante.fr](https://www.mybrocante.fr/m/5280/reglement/6455); [brocabrac.fr](https://brocabrac.fr/evenement/document/d52abe4c5c952934904326b2e7f89baef0404f3e5535bce08e50b9998d731f1a). **I could not verify how fully they cover the register and the attestations. This must be checked before building.**
- MVP: a registration link that collects ID and the sworn statement with e-signature, assigns a pitch on a stand map, and takes payment. On the day, a QR check-in. The app generates the legal register as a PDF for the mairie or prefecture and can flag sellers who went over 2 events, though that check is only possible if data is shared across organisers. Model: free for the association, with a small fee per pitch (€0.50-1) or an optional tip, like HelloAsso.
- WTP: low, so the fee should be paid per transaction.

**C. Phytosanitary register for small or diversified farms (market gardeners, small winegrowers, orchards, organic farms) — PROMISING, with time pressure**
- Pain and regulation: the machine-readable register applies from 1 Jan 2027 across the EU, and paper entries must be converted (see Q2).
- Existing tools: Smag, Geofolia, Mes Parcelles and Farmi suites, and a future Chambres d'agriculture app — [smag.tech guide](https://smag.tech/espace-partenaires/le-petit-guide-du-registre-phyo-smag/); [Cerfrance traceability offer](https://dessavoie.cerfrance.fr/upload/files/document/69bbe1a66a20a2.99598313_flyer_conseil_tracabilite_v4.pdf)
- Gap (inferred): the incumbents are suites built for field crops. Small, diversified farms with many small plots and many crops need a fast entry tool that works in the field without network. It should look up the product and its AMM number from the official database, record the GPS parcel, accept voice entry, and export a compliant file. It could also cover irrigation and organic certification notebooks.
- MVP: an offline PWA with the product catalogue (from the official product database), GPS parcel drawing, voice dictation and Excel/XML export. Price: €5-10 a month, or free with a paid export for organic certification. Main risk: the free Chambres app.
- Market size: not found (number of farms by size is a gap; INSEE and Agreste could provide it).

**D. Placier and market-fee collection for small communes and organisers of night or producers' markets — POSSIBLE (B2G)**
- Pain: receipts from stub books, drawing lots for passagers, and per-metre fees (for example Oullins 2021: €1 per linear metre per ticket, €18 per linear metre per semester; old data) — [Oullins tarifs](https://Oullins.fr/fileadmin/documents/Vie_municipale/Conseil_municipal/Tarifs_communaux/marches_forains_20210507.pdf); [Villedenay](https://villedenay.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/2209-Reglement_marche_VD_signe.pdf)
- Existing tools: Logitud "Régie et Marchés" (back office plus mobile app, QR check-in of traders, drawing lots). Its own documentation says only one commune used the QR check-in when it was written. Berger-Levrault also has an offer — [Logitud mobile doc](https://support.logitud.fr/wp-inside/uploads/documentation/regie_marche/Documentation_Mobile_Regie_et_Marches.pdf); [Berger-Levrault](https://www.berger-levrault.com/app/uploads/sites/2/2023/08/cp-lindispensable-des-marches-de-plein-air.pdf)
- Gap (inferred): enterprise vendors serve medium and large cities. Small communes, summer and night markets, and producers' markets run by associations probably stay on paper. Selling to the public sector is slow (public procurement rules and municipal cash-office rules).
- MVP: a placier phone app (offline) with a stand map, subscriber versus passager handling, on-site drawing of lots, digital receipts by SMS or email, and a daily cash export. A trader-side app could show available pitches and offer pre-booking.

**E. Volunteer shift planning for amateur clubs and associations (buvette, scorers' table, refereeing, car-pooling, events) — POSSIBLE but crowded around the edges**
- Pain: the workload falls on a few officials, as in the 20-year solo-convocations anecdote — [scorenco.com](https://scorenco.com/clubs/). Federations publish guides on planning volunteers — [district-foot-lot.fff.fr](https://district-foot-lot.fff.fr/wp-content/uploads/sites/71/bsk-pdf-manager/ee4bd543e2d2885344706b8b6a3ced7f.pdf)
- Existing tools: Scorenco, SportEasy, AssoConnect, HelloAsso, and generic tools such as Doodle and Google Sheets. Competition is strong. The possible gap is a narrow "rota for parents" feature: a buvette shift with stock and cash count, and a scorers' table rota linked to federation match calendars.
- Verdict: only as a free, viral micro-tool. It would be hard to monetise.

**F. E-invoicing helper for low-digital artisans and market traders (paper or Word to Factur-X, B2C e-reporting from 2027) — POSSIBLE, narrow**
- See Q2. Free approved platforms exist (Pennylane, Qonto, Indy, Tiime, Abby). Readiness is lagging: 1 in 5 TPE had not started in Oct 2026, and only 35% know the content well — [legalplace.fr](https://www.legalplace.fr/actualites/barometre-legalplace-facturation-electronique/); [blog.tiime.fr](https://blog.tiime.fr/facturation-electronique-2026-etude-tpe-france)
- Gap: "the last 20%". These are people who will not adopt a banking or accounting suite. The idea would be an assistant (WhatsApp-like or voice) that turns a photo of a paper invoice or a dictated invoice into a compliant one sent through a partner approved platform. It is a distribution play, perhaps through accountants or trade chambers (CMA, CCI). Risk: the incumbents are free.

**G. Quotes and invoices for building trades — NOT RECOMMENDED (crowded)**
- Obat from €32 excl. VAT a month, Tolteck from €25 excl. VAT a month, Costructor free with no volume limit, Batappli used by 15,000 artisans, and Axonaut from €69.99 excl. VAT a month — [tool-advisor.fr](https://tool-advisor.fr/logiciel-facturation/comparatif/logiciel-devis-facture-batiment/); [independant.io](https://independant.io/avis/batappli/). Remaining frictions: progress billing (situations de travaux) is missing in generic tools like Indy, and each extra user adds about €200 a year — [hackceleration.com](https://hackceleration.com/fr/labs/meilleur/logiciel-facturation-artisans). These are feature gaps, not an empty market.

**H. HACCP and temperature logs for small restaurants and food trucks — NOT RECOMMENDED (crowded, low WTP)**
- Paper remains legal. Apps start at €19.99 excl. VAT a month, and connected-sensor bundles from €119 excl. VAT a month (Octopus HACCP, Yokitup, Frizio, Traqfood, etc.) — [lafabriquedunet.fr](https://www.lafabriquedunet.fr/logiciels/gestion/logiciel-haccp); [hackceleration.com](https://hackceleration.com/fr/labs/meilleur/logiciel-haccp)

**I. Beekeeping registers (registre d'élevage, cahier de miellerie) — NOT RECOMMENDED**
- BeePerf exports the register and is adding extraction, potting and sales traceability. Apitracer Lite is cheap and well reviewed — [App Store BeePerf](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1531124152); [reussir.fr apiculture](https://www.reussir.fr/apiculture/une-application-pour-organiser-son-travail)

**J. Generic "Excel replacement" for SMBs — NOT RECOMMENDED as a framing**
- The "industries still on spreadsheets" content found is SEO or marketing with no clear methodology — [bigideasdb.com](https://bigideasdb.com/industries-still-running-on-spreadsheets). Some owners defend spreadsheets because they are cheap and flexible — [contra.com](https://contra.com/p/ZRPlFlkk-why-excel-still-beats-expensive)

### Inferences
- **Patterns that make a niche promising:**
  - A legal obligation with a format the user has little control over: register, attestation, export.
  - Fragmented, part-time or volunteer users.
  - Field conditions: markets, fields, car parks, which call for offline mobile use and photo or voice input.
  - Incumbents priced per user at €30 a month or more, or sold to cities through enterprise sales.
- **Shared MVP core:** a "compliant register" engine (tamper-evident log, sequential numbering, ID or photo capture, OCR, PDF or XML export for the authorities). It could be reused across niches A, B, C and D. That suggests a horizontal "registres obligatoires pour petites structures" product with vertical templates.
- **Monetisation:** fees per event or transaction (B, D) are more realistic than subscriptions. Subscriptions of €5-10 a month suit A and C.
- **Skepticism:** before building, check how far Brocabrac, MyBrocante and HelloAsso already cover niche B, and what the free Chambres d'agriculture app covers in niche C.
